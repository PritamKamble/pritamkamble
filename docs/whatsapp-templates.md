# WhatsApp quick-reply templates

Save each of these as a Quick Reply in WhatsApp Business (Settings → Business tools → Quick replies) with a short `/shortcut`. Fill in `[brackets]` before sending — never send a template unedited, it should read like you actually typed it.

---

## Candidate — first reply after they confirm on WhatsApp

`/candidate-hi`

```
Hey [Name]! Got your application — you're #[X] in line for the Full-Stack + GenAI batch.

Quick q before we hop on a call: what's pulling you toward this — first dev role, or switching from something else?
```

Why open with a question instead of a pitch: it turns a broadcast-feeling confirmation into a real conversation immediately, and their answer tells you which track/angle to lead with on the call.

---

## Candidate — booking the fit call

`/candidate-call`

```
Cool, makes sense. Let's do a quick 15-min call so I can hear more and you can ask anything — no pressure either way, just want to see if this is actually a fit for where you're at.

Free at [2-3 specific time options] this week? Or grab a slot directly: [Calendly link]
```

Always offer 2–3 specific times *or* the Calendly link — making them guess your availability adds friction that kills momentum.

---

## Candidate — after the call, enrolling them

`/candidate-enroll`

```
Great talking today, [Name]. Based on what you shared, I think [Track] is the right fit — starting with [specific first-week thing, e.g. "the DSA foundations + Git workflow"].

I'll get your portal access set up — you'll get a signup link at this number/email. Once you're in, submit your first daily log within 48h so we keep momentum from today's call.

Any questions before we start?
```

The 48h nudge matters — the daily-log habit is easiest to start while the conversation is still fresh; waiting a week to log for the first time is a common drop-off point.

---

## Candidate — gentle re-engagement (went quiet after applying)

`/candidate-followup`

```
Hey [Name] — following up on your application from [date]. Still interested in the batch, or has the timing changed on your end?

No worries either way, just closing the loop.
```

Send this once, roughly 5–7 days after silence following your first message. Don't send a second follow-up unless they respond — repeated unprompted messages to the same person is exactly the pattern that reads as spammy (to them, and to WhatsApp's own spam detection).

---

## Employer — first reply after they message about hiring

`/employer-hi`

```
Hey [Name], thanks for reaching out! Quick context: every candidate in the pool goes through a 24-week mentor-led program I run personally — real shipped projects, weekly mock interviews I score myself.

What kind of role are you looking to fill, and what stack/experience level?
```

Same principle as the candidate opener — ask before pitching, so your next message can speak directly to their actual need instead of a generic overview.

---

## Employer — after they've signed up, no activity yet

`/employer-nudge`

```
Hey [Name] — saw you created an employer account. Happy to jump on a quick call and walk you through the candidate pool directly if that's easier than browsing solo, or send over 2-3 profiles that match what you mentioned you're hiring for.

Whichever's more useful — just say the word.
```

Employers who sign up and see an empty-feeling dashboard are a real drop-off risk (flagged in an earlier review) — this message exists specifically to catch that gap with a human touch instead of leaving them to self-serve alone.

---

## General rules

- **Never send the same unedited template to more than one person in a row** — always adjust at least the opening line so it reads as written for them, not copy-pasted.
- **One follow-up, not a sequence** — if there's no reply after the single re-engagement message, let it go. Repeated unprompted messages are the pattern that gets numbers flagged as spam.
- **Reply fast to inbound** — since your funnel is built to get people to message you first, response speed *is* your conversion rate. Aim to reply within the hour during waking hours.
