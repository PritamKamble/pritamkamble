Before you apply

Is this beginner-friendly?

Yes. We start from fundamentals and accelerate into projects fast. Prior basics help but aren't required.

What do I need to get started?

A laptop with 8GB+ RAM, stable internet, VS Code, and a GitHub account.

Do I get recordings of live sessions?

Yes — every live session is recorded, plus you get notes and assignments to work through async.

How do referrals actually work?

Once you hit milestone scores across projects and mocks, relevant referrals and drive links get shared directly with you.

Is there a certificate?

Yes, on completing the program and presenting your capstone demo.

What if I fall behind or it's not working out for me?

Since it's 1-on-1, we adjust pace to your schedule first — that's the whole point of not being a fixed-cohort course. But if you commit to the weekly cadence and you're still not shipping real, working code by week 3, we sit down and fix the plan together, or you walk — no hard feelings. This only works if it works for you.
