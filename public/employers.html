// faq

Before you sign up

Is this really free?

Yes. No listing fees, no placement commission. This is a distribution channel for mentor-trained candidates, not a recruiting agency.

How are candidates vetted?

Every candidate goes through a structured 24-week program with weekly mock interviews and project reviews. Progress data on the portal is self-reported by candidates but visible in aggregate, and Pritam personally mentors the top performers.

What roles can I post?

Full-time, internship, or contract roles in software engineering — Full-Stack, GenAI/AI Engineering, or general junior dev roles.

How fast will I get applicants?

Depends on batch size and role fit — the pool is actively growing. Reach out on WhatsApp for a faster, hands-on match if you need to fill a role urgently.

Create a free account and start browsing — or message Pritam directly if you're hiring urgently.
